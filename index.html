Our story

A loom takes four days to say what a machine says in four minutes.

We think that difference is worth paying for. Taana was started in 2019 after a visit to a weaving village in Nadia district, where handloom sarees were being sold for less than the cost of the yarn. We now work directly with weaver cooperatives, pay above market rate, and put the weaver's name on every piece we sell.

"My mother wove on this same loom. Taana is the first place that ever asked me to sign my work."— Rina Das, Shantipur weaving cluster
